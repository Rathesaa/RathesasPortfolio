Project 01 · Scenario-based learning

Cybersecurity Basics for Remote Workers

A short digital learning concept that helps employees recognize suspicious messages and practise safer responses in realistic workplace situations.

At a glance

Articulate Rise / H5P concept · Scenario writing · Feedback design

The learning problem

Employees may recognize the word phishing but still feel unsure when deciding what to do with an unexpected or suspicious message.

Target audience

Employees who work remotely or use email and shared digital documents as part of their daily work. The concept assumes a general workplace audience; it can be adapted after a learner analysis.

Learning objectives

By the end of this learning experience, learners should be able to:

  • Identify common warning signs in suspicious messages.
  • Choose an appropriate action when an email or shared document seems unusual.
  • Describe what to do after interacting with a suspicious link.

Why this learning method?

Scenario-based learning gives learners a chance to apply information in context. Retrieval practice and immediate feedback help them check their understanding and learn from each decision.

Design and development

The proposed module uses short workplace scenarios, multiple-choice decisions, feedback after each response, and a completion screen. The scenarios cover an urgent payroll request, an unexpected shared document, and responding after entering a password on a suspicious page.

Assessment and evaluation

Learners choose a response for each scenario. Feedback explains why the selected action is or is not appropriate. A fuller version could include a short pre-check and a follow-up knowledge check after a delay.

Design reflection

The key design decision is to focus on action rather than asking learners to memorize a list of phishing definitions. In a real deployment, the scenarios and recommended reporting steps would need review by the organization’s IT/security team.
