Project 03 · UX + Adaptive Learning

LearnFlow: A More Personalized Learning Experience

An adaptive learning management system concept that explores how learner preferences, activity, and knowledge-check performance can help create more relevant and supportive workplace learning experiences.

01 · The challenge

One learning experience does not work equally well for everyone

Traditional learning platforms often present the same content in the same format to every learner. However, employees may have different preferences, levels of prior knowledge, schedules, and support needs.

The design problem

How might we create a learning platform that responds to individual preferences and learning progress, helping employees engage with content in ways that support their development?

Learner needs

Employees need flexibility in how they access information, clear learning goals, relevant practice, useful feedback, and visibility into their progress.

Design opportunity

Use learner-selected preferences and demonstrated learning needs to suggest appropriate content formats, activities, and additional support.

02 · Audience & objectives

Designing around individual learners

LearnFlow is intended for employees balancing required workplace training with optional professional development.

01

Assigned learners

Employees completing required training who need a clear view of deadlines, progress, and outstanding activities.

02

Self-directed learners

Employees exploring optional learning opportunities to build skills and support career development.

03

Different learning needs

Employees who benefit from different explanations, examples, practice activities, and levels of guidance.

Experience objectives

✓Allow learners to communicate their preferred ways of engaging with content.
✓Present learning through a choice of instructional methods.
✓Use knowledge checks to identify topics that may need reinforcement.
✓Adjust learning suggestions based on preferences and demonstrated progress.
03 · Experience strategy

From a standard LMS to an adaptive experience

The platform combines learner choice with ongoing feedback. Instead of assigning people a fixed learning-style label, it gives them control over content formats and responds to their activity.

◎

Understand learner preferences

Ask learners how they prefer to explore information, the length of sessions they prefer, and the types of support they find useful.

▧

Offer multiple instructional methods

Provide written explanations, visual examples, demonstrations, scenarios, and hands-on practice where appropriate.

↗

Respond to learning progress

Use knowledge-check performance and learner feedback to suggest review, additional examples, or more challenging activities.

✧

Keep the learner in control

Make recommendations optional, explain why they appear, and allow learners to change preferences whenever they want.

A note on learning styles

LearnFlow does not classify people into fixed visual, auditory, or kinaesthetic learning types. It explores self-reported preferences and observed learning needs. Different instructional methods can support different objectives, and learners should be free to use more than one method.

05 · How adaptive learning could work

A learning experience that responds over time

In a future implementation, an adaptive recommendation engine could combine learner-selected preferences with performance and engagement data to suggest relevant learning activities.

1. Understand the learner

Collect optional preferences, learning goals, selected content formats, and preferred session length.

2. Observe learning activity

Review knowledge-check results, course progress, attempts, learner feedback, and topics that may need additional support.

3. Recommend a learning method

Suggest a relevant combination of explanations, examples, demonstrations, practice, and review activities.

4. Refine recommendations

Use new activity and learner feedback to improve suggestions while allowing learners to change or ignore them.

Example adaptive response

If a learner struggles with a knowledge check, the system could offer a simpler explanation, a worked example, or another practice activity. It should not assume that one incorrect answer means the learner has a particular learning style or ability.

06 · Evaluation

How could the experience be evaluated?

The concept would need usability testing and learner feedback before being considered for implementation.

Task completion

Can learners set preferences, locate courses, switch content formats, and identify their next learning activity?

Learning progress

Do knowledge checks show improvement over time? Are learners able to apply what they have practised?

Learner experience

Do employees find recommendations relevant, understandable, and easy to control?

07 · Reflection

Personalized learning should respond to people, not put them into boxes.

LearnFlow explores how user-centred design and adaptive learning principles can support more flexible workplace learning. By combining learner preferences with progress and knowledge-check feedback, the platform could help employees access relevant explanations and practice. The goal is not to assign a permanent learning style, but to give learners more choice and provide useful support as their needs change.
